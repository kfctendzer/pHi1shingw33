About Joe

Born in 1988, Joe grew up in a small town dreaming of adventure. After a decade in tech, he sold everything and hit the road. He's documented over 50 countries with zero sponsorships – just pure passion.

Fun fact: Joe once hitchhiked 2,000 miles across Australia in 3 weeks!
